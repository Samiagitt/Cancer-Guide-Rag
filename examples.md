## Question 1
What is cancer in simple words?
**Answer:**
Cancer is a disease where some of the body's cells grow and multiply uncontrollably. Normally, cells grow, divide, and die in an organized way, but in cancer, this process breaks down, leading to abnormal cells surviving and new cells forming when they are not needed.

## Question 2

What is chemotherapy?

**Answer:**
Chemotherapy is a cancer treatment that uses medicines to kill cancer cells or slow their growth. It may be used alone or with other treatments such as surgery, radiation therapy, immunotherapy, or targeted therapy.
